Frequently Asked Questions

Find immediate answers to our most common customer inquiries below.

How do I track the shipping progress of my order?

Once your order is processed, you will receive an email confirmation with an automated tracking link. You can also use our Package Tracking Tool above by entering your Order Reference ID (e.g., #QM-84920).

What are the shipping costs and estimated transit times?

Standard delivery is completely free on all orders over $50. For orders under $50, a flat shipping fee of $4.99 applies. Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days, while Express Priority shipping delivers within 1-2 business days.

What is your 30-day return and exchange policy?

We want you to love your purchase. If you are not completely satisfied, you can return any item within 30 days of delivery in original packaging for a full refund or replacement. No restocking fees are ever charged.

Which payment methods and currencies do you accept?

We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and UPI / Cash on Delivery where supported. All transactions are protected by bank-grade 256-bit SSL encryption.

Can I modify or cancel my order after placing it?

Orders can be modified or cancelled within 2 hours of placement before they enter the warehouse packing stage. Please contact our 24/7 support line or message us via Live Chat immediately with your order number.

How do I redeem promotional codes like QMART20?

In your Shopping Cart page, enter your promo code into the "Have a discount coupon?" box and click "Apply". Valid codes such as QMART20 will immediately deduct the discount from your subtotal.

What should I do if my package arrives damaged or missing?

Please take a clear photo of the damaged package and reach out to our Customer Care team at support@qmart.com. We will arrange a complimentary immediate replacement or full refund within 24 hours.
